PRIMARY FREIGHT TOOLKIT

Privacy Policy

Primary Freight Toolkit — the Five Nines Gmail add-on and connected load-intake workflow.

Effective September 30, 2026

1. Who operates the app

Five Nines Logistics LLC ("Five Nines," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Primary Freight Toolkit for authorized business users. This policy covers information handled through the Gmail add-on, its load-intake web app, and the resulting dashboard handoff. It does not replace notices for unrelated company websites, employee records, customer portals, or third-party services.

Contact us about privacy at chris@shipfivenines.com.

2. Information the workflow handles

  • Account and access information: your Google account email, identity identifiers and, where supplied for dashboard sign-in, your name and profile information. Authentication tokens and session information establish your identity and authorized access. The Toolkit does not ask you to give Five Nines your Google password.
  • Selected Gmail content: the subject and link for the conversation open in Gmail may be used for context. When you choose the conversation-analysis workflow, the app reads the available messages in that conversation, including sender and recipient details, dates, subjects, and message text. That can include quoted or forwarded material and signatures. It is not an inbox-wide background scanner.
  • Information you enter or upload: pasted text, manual load details, PDF or image tenders, screenshots, file names and file metadata. Only provide material you are authorized to use for freight operations.
  • Work product: saved draft content, transcriptions, extracted fields, source excerpts, missing-data warnings, revision history, your corrections and confirmations, source links, submission status, and related load references.
  • Operational information: timestamps, request and error information, authentication and security events, and technical information processed by our hosting providers, which can include network and device information.

Freight source material can contain contact details, pickup and delivery locations, commercial rates, shipment instructions, and other confidential business information. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, medical information, or unrelated personal information.

3. When information is saved and used

We use this information to authenticate approved users, save and recover working drafts, transcribe documents, propose load fields, show supporting evidence, process your edits, and send a reviewed draft to the dashboard when you choose that action. We also use necessary operational information to troubleshoot failures, protect accounts, investigate abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Storage is not delayed until final submission: entering or uploading material into the durable intake workflow can save it as a working draft. In the Gmail workflow, the selected conversation is saved before AI extraction so it can be recovered if the window closes. Review what you include before starting.

4. AI processing

When you request transcription or analysis, the relevant source material is sent to Google's Gemini service through Vertex AI in our configured Google Cloud project. A document-analysis request can include the uploaded file; conversation analysis includes the selected conversation's text and metadata. Model output is saved with the working draft for your review.

Five Nines does not use Google user data from this app to train or fine-tune generalized AI models. Google's service documentation describes restrictions on model training with customer data and circumstances where processing data can be retained, including abuse monitoring. We do not promise zero retention by Google. See Google's AI data-handling documentation.

AI results can be incomplete or wrong. A person must check them against the source before operational use.

5. Access, sharing, and service providers

Working drafts: normal draft access is tied to the authenticated user's account. They are not publicly listed. Authorized infrastructure administrators can have technical access needed to operate, secure, or support the service; account-specific access does not mean the data is inaccessible to administrators.

Dashboard submission: when you deliberately submit a draft, its extracted information, evidence excerpts, source link, and submitting-account information enter the shared Primary Freight dashboard queue. Other staff already authorized for that dashboard can review it. This is not necessarily limited to Five Nines users or to the person who created the draft. Submitting a draft is a sharing step, not a private note to yourself.

Our service providers support the following functions:

  • Google: Workspace and Apps Script execution, Google sign-in, AI processing, and optional Drive exports.
  • Fly.io: the authenticated dashboard and intake API, including the operational database used for submitted drafts and load-entry records.
  • Supabase: durable working-draft records and private uploaded-file storage, accessed through our server.

When you request a Drive export, the app creates a separate copy in your Drive. Its later access and retention follow your Drive sharing choices and Workspace controls. The app does not automatically publish that copy. A later, separately authorized dashboard workflow may transmit approved shipment data to TAI; analyzing or handing off a draft does not itself book a shipment or send an email.

We restrict human review of Google-derived content to your affirmative agreement to review specific material, necessary security or abuse investigation, or legal requirements. Support requests should identify the particular draft or content you authorize us to inspect. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for credit or lending decisions.

6. Google API data commitments

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google-derived data is used for the visible app functions described here, not unrelated advertising, profiling, or general-purpose model training. Any transfer must be permitted by that policy and applicable law.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved drafts, source files, extracted information, and submitted operational records are retained to support freight work, recovery, audit needs, dispute handling, security, and applicable recordkeeping requirements. The current workflow does not automatically purge saved drafts or uploaded files after a fixed number of days. Removal requires authorized administrative action.

We assess retention using whether the record is still needed for an active shipment, accounting or business recordkeeping, a legal requirement or hold, a dispute, or a security investigation. Information no longer needed for those purposes should be removed through our administrative process. The approved retention schedule and deletion process must be maintained by Five Nines.

Closing the browser, removing the add-on, signing out, or revoking Google access does not by itself erase already saved records. Request deletion by emailing chris@shipfivenines.com with your business email and relevant draft or load reference. We verify authority, identify related records, and explain any lawful reason some information must remain. Copies in backups, logs, Google Drive, Gmail, TAI, or other recipients' systems can require separate handling and may follow different retention periods.

8. Security and browser storage

The workflow uses encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated service requests, account checks for working drafts, private file storage, and restricted administrative access. The dashboard uses session cookies and browser storage needed for sign-in and app operation. These policy and support pages do not load advertising trackers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; protect your account and report unexpected access promptly.

Google permission grants and company access approval are separate controls. Revoking a permission can disable a feature without deleting existing business records. You can review Google connections in your Google Account; company-managed installations may also require administrator action.

9. Your requests and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, request access or a copy, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, or object to or restrict certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent for future processing. Contact the address above; we may need to verify your identity or authority and will handle requests within applicable legal requirements. We will not require you to disclose your password to make a request.

The app is intended for authorized business users, not for children or public consumer registration. Cloud processing may occur outside your state or country, depending on the provider and configuration; this policy does not promise a particular data-residency location.

10. Changes and contact

We will post policy updates here with a revised effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for a material change. A policy update does not itself authorize an unrelated use of previously collected Google data.
