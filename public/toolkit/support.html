PRIMARY FREIGHT TOOLKIT

Support

Help with Primary Freight Toolkit, saved drafts, and dashboard handoff.

Updated September 30, 2026 · Operator: Five Nines Logistics LLC.

Contact

Email chris@shipfivenines.com. Use the subject Primary Freight Toolkit support. Support is handled by the company administrator; no 24/7 coverage or guaranteed response time is promised.

For an urgent shipment, use your established dispatch or operations escalation process. Do not rely on an app-support email for an immediate operational response.

What to include

  • Your approved work email and the approximate time of the problem, including time zone.
  • The feature you were using and a short description of what you expected and what happened.
  • A draft or load reference, if available, and the exact error message.
  • A cropped or redacted screenshot if it helps. Remove unrelated emails, financial information, personal information, and credentials.

Never send passwords, two-step verification codes, API keys, authentication tokens, or a full mailbox export. If investigation requires reading source content, identify the specific draft or file you authorize support to inspect. We will not ask for access to your whole mailbox to troubleshoot one draft.

Start here

  1. Sign in to Gmail with the work account approved by your administrator.
  2. After the administrator has installed and enabled the app, open Primary Freight Toolkit from Gmail's right sidebar.
  3. For email analysis, open the relevant conversation and choose the conversation workflow. For a tender, screenshot, or manual entry, use the corresponding source option.
  4. Wait for a confirmed saved state. Review transcribed text and extracted fields against the original source.
  5. Confirm the fields, then deliberately send the draft to the dashboard. Open the dashboard with an approved account and finish load entry there.

The resulting load-entry form starts as a Quote with Send to TAI off. Intake analysis and handoff do not automatically book a load or send email.

Common problems

I cannot see the app in Gmail

Confirm the signed-in account and expand the right sidebar. Ask the administrator whether installation is complete and your account is included. Do not install an unrelated similarly named app.

I receive an access-denied message

Google installation, intake approval, and dashboard access are separate. Confirm that you are using the intended account and contact support. Do not share another employee's session or ask for broad project-administrator access.

A draft is missing or saving failed

Check Saved drafts using the same account that created it. A working draft is account-specific. Keep your source material until the app confirms it was saved. If the result is uncertain, check for an existing draft or submission before starting another one.

AI read the tender incorrectly

Compare the transcription and fields to the original document. Correct errors and resolve missing or conflicting information before confirmation. A file uploaded to Toolkit intake must be a supported PDF, PNG, or JPEG and no larger than 10 MB. This intake limit is separate from the customer/carrier website portals' document-upload limits.

The dashboard link opens sign-in instead of the draft

Sign in with the approved dashboard account, then reopen the draft link. Do not change the draft identifier or use another person's account.

The app says the conversation or file is too large

Do not assume a partial extraction is complete. Use a supported, smaller source while preserving the relevant instructions and revisions, or complete the load through the normal manual workflow.

Privacy, correction, and deletion requests

Email chris@shipfivenines.com with the subject Toolkit privacy request. Describe the request, identify the relevant account and record, and state whether it concerns a working draft, uploaded source, submitted dashboard record, or Drive export. Do not attach sensitive source material unless requested through an approved channel.

We may need to verify your identity or authority and check business, security, and legal retention requirements. Removing the add-on or revoking access does not automatically delete stored records. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Security concerns

If you suspect unauthorized access, contact the administrator promptly with the subject Toolkit security concern. Stop sharing the affected material and follow company incident procedures. Do not send secrets or exploit instructions through an unsecured support message.
