3. What the Toolkit does

The Toolkit helps turn a selected email conversation, pasted or manually entered text, or an uploaded tender or screenshot into an editable freight draft. It can save working drafts, use AI to propose structured information, show source evidence, and send a reviewed draft to the authenticated Primary Freight dashboard. It also provides links to other dashboard tools; their availability depends on existing permissions and services.

Analysis is not a booking. Creating, analyzing, confirming, or handing off an intake draft does not by itself tender freight, book a carrier, dispatch a load, issue a binding rate confirmation, send an email, or create a shipment in TAI. Dashboard load entry starts as a Quote with Send to TAI off. Any later booking, communication, or system write requires a separate authorized action in the applicable workflow.

4. Your review responsibilities

AI transcription and extraction can omit information, misread numbers, mix revisions, or make other errors. You are responsible for checking the source documents and conversation and correcting the draft before using it operationally.

  • Verify the customer and carrier, all stops, addresses, dates, times and time zones, equipment, commodity, weight, quantity, and references.
  • Distinguish the customer sell rate from the carrier buy rate. Confirm fuel, accessorials, and special terms separately.
  • Resolve missing information, conflicting instructions, rejected offers, and cancellations. Do not treat a proposed rate as an accepted rate.
  • Verify carrier qualification and required approvals through the company's established procedures. The Toolkit is not a substitute for those checks.
  • Before retrying an uncertain submission or booking, check whether the prior attempt already succeeded. Avoid duplicate loads and duplicate communications.
